Read on this build

why the numbers are where they are

Badge plan

Tokens and requirements are exact —the per-attribute token ladders are scraped for all 45 position/height frames and confirmed against a live readout, and the requirements are the engine's own table. Two things are not: the tier costs 3/5/6/7 rest on two live samples (Breaker and Float Game) and do not accumulate —Gold is 6, not 3+5+6. If a badge prices differently it can be corrected per badge. And the number of badge slots per discipline was never extracted, so this plan is limited by tokens alone. If the game also caps slots, your real loadout is a subset of this one. A fused badge costs no tokens —you get them back —so the solver iterates: equip, fuse, refund, re-equip. That reaches a fixed point but is not a proof of optimality; a jointly exact solve is a three-resource problem too slow to run on every drag.

Build for badges

Pick the tier you want on up to four badges per category, then press Build these. Each pick becomes an attribute target in the builder, so you can see and edit every number before searching. Tiers your position can never reach are struck through rather than hidden. The small number on a tier is what it actually asks for —the hardest leg of an and , the cheapest leg of an or .

Animations

Checked against this build including cap breakers —a broken 96 Driving Dunk unlocks what a natural 96 does, confirmed in game. Height is its own answer: an animation outside your frame's band is not a number you can chase.

Build names

Rarity is measured, not guessed: the count is how many of the game ’s 137,959 attribute profiles earn that name, read from the same table that names your build.

Where cap breakers pay best

The park is exact arithmetic off the game ’s weights table. Collecting it means the finished build landing on that player type, which is a search problem —so these read up to . Click a row and the finder will try to build it.

Ceilings, cap breaker ladders, the constraint graph, badge requirements and the badge-token ladders are read out of NBA 2K27's own tuning tables (tune 286298702 ) —not fitted or estimated. Ladders reproduce the engine on 100,000 slots; Overall on 200,000 builds; a full live build matched 21/21 ceilings and 21/21 ladders . The tables ship with the page, so the builder computes in your browser rather than asking a server. (Accounts and the premium solvers are the exception —those need the network.)
